Instagram Non-Reciprocal Followers

Find out who you follow that doesn't follow you back. 100% in your browser.

Your data never leaves this page. This tool runs entirely client-side. No files are uploaded, no cookies are set, no analytics, no servers.
How to use this tool
  1. Open Instagram → Settings & Privacy → Accounts Center.
  2. Go to Your information and permissions → Download your information.
  3. Choose Some of your information, then check only Followers and following.
  4. Set the format to JSON and the date range to All time, then tap Create files.
  5. Wait 5–15 minutes. Meta will email you a download link. Download and extract the ZIP.
  6. Inside the extracted folder, find followers_1.json and following.json.
  7. Upload both files below, then click Find Who Doesn't Follow Back.
Tip: Only followers_1.json and following.json are needed. You can ignore every other file in the export ZIP.
Heads up: Don't mass-unfollow. Instagram rate-limits around 30–50 unfollows per hour. Spread your cleanup across a couple of sessions to avoid a temporary action block.
